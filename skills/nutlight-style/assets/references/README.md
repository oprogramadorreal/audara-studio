# Nutlight: the reference images

Eight images that show the Nutlight style: what it should look like and how good it should be. A video in
the style is drawn in code; these guide it and never appear in it. They were made by the audara-studio
author with ChatGPT's image model and are part of the plugin under its license (MIT). They are stills, so
motion is described in words below.

The two descriptions are the author's, kept close to their words. After them, each image has a line on what
it shows and the kinds of scene it guides. The places and stories in the images are examples. A new video
builds its own settings and casts its own squirrels.

## The look, in the author's words

A cinematic geometric illustration aesthetic, in the tradition of animated science explainers, brings
together expressive animal characters, carefully structured environments and the visual clarity of a good
explainer. Its geometric foundation is enriched by atmospheric lighting, soft dimensional shading and an
expansive sense of space. The images have the character of elaborate 2D animation frames, with precise
graphic construction and controlled 2.5D depth.

Circles, ellipses, rounded rectangles, arcs, wedges and flowing curves form a unified vocabulary.
Characters have broad, readable silhouettes. Architecture and machinery emerge from combinations of
repeated modules, sweeping structural lines and purposeful geometric detail. Contours are crisp and smooth.
Large colour regions carry much of the visual information, with smaller marks concentrated around faces,
interfaces, mechanisms and other focal subjects.

Volume comes from soft internal gradients, broad shadow planes, gentle contact shadows, reflected colour
and narrow highlights. A squirrel's forehead curves through several warm tones, a tail curls through nested
bands of colour, and a metallic platform catches a simplified reflection. Glass appears through
transparency and luminous edges; polished surfaces carry clear, restrained reflections. The dimensionality
stays illustrative, with clean shapes visible beneath the shading.

The squirrels have large rounded heads, compact pear-shaped bodies, upright ears with darker inner shapes,
soft cheeks and short rounded limbs. Oversized dark eyes sit within pale cream surrounds. Tiny dark noses,
short cream muzzles and simple curved smiles give their faces an open, friendly expression. Cream areas
continue from the face into the chest and belly. Small forepaws rest or gesture naturally, while broad hind
paws support the compact bodies.

Their most distinctive feature is the enormous curled tail. Each tail rises behind the body in a broad
C-shaped or spiral silhouette, often rivalling the torso in size. Sweeping bands of related colours describe
its curve and thickness. In these images one squirrel has amber and orange fur, a smaller one warmer
red-orange and peach tones, a third sandy tan fur with a deeper caramel tail. Each video casts its own, and
their warm identities stay recognisable under every light.

Expression is carried by gaze, head angle, ear position, slight changes in the mouth, and the relationship
between the paws and the surrounding objects. A frontal face has a centred nose and balanced eyes. A gentle
three-quarter face has an offset muzzle, a broader near cheek and a narrower far eye. The expressions feel
curious, attentive, relaxed or quietly delighted.

The palette changes with the setting. Computing interiors have ink-navy, indigo, charcoal and teal surfaces
punctuated by cyan light. Sunset landscapes unfold through honey, orange, peach, pink and violet. Golden
laboratories combine champagne architecture, amber illumination, graphite machinery and warm ivory
surfaces. Blue cities range from icy cyan and periwinkle to saturated cobalt and midnight blue. Local colour
and environmental light work together, with warm characters standing out against cooler or darker
surroundings.

Widescreen compositions have a clear hierarchy and a strong sense of depth. Foreground plants, furniture,
structural edges or rounded platforms frame the main action. Characters and important mechanisms occupy the
middle of the visual story, while distant buildings, mountains, stars and landscapes become softer and less
contrasty. Broad skies, quiet surfaces and open spaces balance intricate clusters of detail.

Technology has an elegant, tangible form: server racks with visible bays and fans, suspended processor
layers, translucent control panels, interconnected nodes, modular habitats and illuminated data paths.
Technical displays carry graphic bars, simple punctuation, charts and geometric symbols. Natural
environments share the same construction through curved shorelines, layered tree silhouettes, broad leaf
shapes and simplified reflections.

Across domestic scenes, futuristic laboratories, landscapes, portraits and conceptual science imagery, the
atmosphere is imaginative, inviting and thoughtful. Warm character appeal and organized technical
complexity belong to the same coherent illustrated world.

## The motion, in the author's words

The animation language is measured, expressive and closely connected to the clean geometry of the artwork.
Small blinks, gentle breathing, slight ear movements and brief changes in gaze give the squirrels a quiet
sense of life. Paw gestures are clear and economical. A head turn reveals the established facial volume,
while the large curled tail moves as a coherent rounded form.

Camera movement has a calm cinematic rhythm. Slow approaches reveal expressions and mechanisms; gentle
lateral movement separates foreground, middle ground and background through parallax. Portraits have a more
settled camera presence, letting the face and posture carry the moment. Wide environmental shots reveal the
scale of the surrounding architecture or landscape.

Secondary movement belongs to the setting. Data packets travel along illuminated connections, holographic
diagrams turn gradually, processor layers move in precise alignment, and distant trains glide through the
city. In natural scenes, selected leaves stir, grass shifts lightly, and small changes in reflection animate
the water. Light stays tied to its source, with restrained pulses around active displays and machinery.

The visual continuity is stable. Facial proportions, tail patterns, colours, clothing, architecture and
object relationships keep their identities throughout a shot. The motion has the clarity of carefully
arranged 2D animation layers combined with selective depth and gently dimensional environments.
